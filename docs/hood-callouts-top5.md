# Hood callouts — top 5 for an in-person visit + video

**For:** Elijah  
**Source:** `data/Raw_data.json` → `submissions[]` (`hood`, `note`).  
**Snapshot:** 1,750 submissions; 1,470 notes with real text (280 empty / `n/a` / one-word yes-no ignored).  
**Not used:** `ratings[]`, `surveys[]` (those key off `building`, not `hood`, and are a different instrument). Duplicate copies at `Reddit_LocalTool/Raw_data.json` and `Reddit_LocalTool/data/Raw_data.json` were ignored.

## Method (short)

- Grouped by exact `hood` string. Same person submitting multiple notes in one hood was counted once per theme.
- Clustered only from note text (keyword + read-through). Amenity name-drops (“gym, pool”) and “close to campus” are **not** issue themes.
- A theme is listed only if **≥ 3 distinct people** in that hood mention the same problem.
- Wampus.fyi walk-time templates (`Walk time to campus is … Data from Wampus.fyi`) were excluded from clustering. They inflate volume without adding issue texture.
- Quotes are shortened, anonymized snippets from the notes. Nothing below is invented.

**“Best” for video** = recurring 3+ voices + filmable / concrete problems + enough texture + a narrative hook. Prefer stacked themes, or one issue that is unusually consistent and vivid.

## Ranked top 5

### 1. Rise at West Campus

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hood** | `Rise at West Campus` |
| **Notes / people** | 54 non-empty notes from **47 people**. 3 people were Wampus-only → **44 people** used for clustering. |

**Recurring issues (3+ people)**

- **Water / plumbing outages — 19 people.** The loudest single cluster in the whole file.  
  - “No water for two weeks and got very insignificant refund. No bottled water was provided”  
  - “didn’t have hot water for a week” / “you don’t have hot water half the time” / “No running water twice this academic year”
- **Elevators — 8.** “Broken elevators (always)”; “elevators are pretty slow on busy hours.”
- **Management / office — 5** (all negative). “unfriendly management”; “front office is HORRIBLE at getting anything done”; compensation after outages called lazy / uneven ($200 vs “some people got 3 months rent”).
- **AC / HVAC — 4.** “Broken A/C for a month”; “AC didn’t work for three months”; “ac is cooked as i type this.”
- **Room size / no-window units — 4.** Small rooms, windowless units, lease not disclosing size differences.
- **Cleanliness / trash — 3.** Trash chute “always broken”; move-in floors “covered in paint splatters and generally just gross”; “smells like shit.”

**Why this is a strong video candidate**  
Water is not a one-off gripe — nearly half the clustered voices mention outages, no hot water, or pressure/temp failure, often with a compensation fight attached. That is a clear A-roll hook (showers, bottled water, trash chute, paint-splattered floors, dead AC) instead of another generic “elevators suck” building.

**Good / bad / vibe**

- **Good:** Close to campus; gym / pool / sauna / jacuzzi get mentioned as actually nice; a few people say maintenance is fast once something is ticketed; electricity-only utilities.
- **Bad:** Recurring water shutoffs (days to two weeks); AC and “something essential” breaking after breaks; windowless / smaller rooms not flagged up front; trash chute and move-in filth; named-manager anger and “SCAM” language; water damage left for months in at least one 4x4.
- **Vibe:** New-looking amenities sitting on top of a reliability problem. Residents sound tired, not nitpicky. Best shoot if you want a “why does a West Campus high-rise keep losing water?” story.

### 2. Tradition on Rio

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hood** | `Tradition on Rio` |
| **Notes / people** | 34 non-empty notes from **33 people**. 4 Wampus-only → **29 people** used for clustering. |

**Recurring issues (3+ people)**

- **Elevators — 16 people.** Unusually consistent: “always broken,” “1/2 elevators always broken,” “broken for most of semester,” “not planning on fixing it.”
- **Building condition / always-breaking — 6.** Uneven floors, torn carpet, “old building terribly maintained,” “something is always breaking,” broken lights/furniture on arrival.
- **Pests / roaches — 4.** “roaches and something is always breaking”; “broken elevator, roaches, dirty”; “Roach infestation when we moved in” (and a claim management tried to pin it on residents).
- **Tour vs actual room — 3.** Tour unit bigger/nicer than the room they got; “Rooms are much smaller than what was shown to us when touring.”
- **Management problems — 3** (plus 5 others who *praise* staff — mixed, not a one-note pile-on). Negative: “New management sucks”; staff “very uninformed”; “tried to pin the roach issue on us.”

**Why this is a strong video candidate**  
The building already shot its own B-roll: a renovated lobby/leasing office sitting in front of broken elevators, roaches, and a “disgusting” older stack. That lobby-vs-hallway contrast is filmable in one walk-through, and 16 elevator voices means you will not be hunting for someone who noticed.

**Good / bad / vibe**

- **Good:** Several people like the staff and the *new* lobby / gym / study area; maintenance is often described as quick on non-elevator stuff; close to campus; a few “big rooms.”
- **Bad:** Elevators treated as a permanent condition; roaches on move-in; bait-and-switch tour rooms; hidden/signing/application fees (~$200 to apply); alley noise; dirty / old building behind the renovation.
- **Vibe:** “They spent the money on the lobby.” Residents sound specifically betrayed (tour, roaches, elevator they “aren’t planning on fixing”), which plays well on camera if you can get a hallway + lobby walk.

### 3. Signature 1909

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hood** | `Signature 1909` |
| **Notes / people** | 23 non-empty notes from **23 people**. 1 Wampus-only → **22 people** used for clustering. |

**Recurring issues (3+ people)**

- **Elevators — 12 people.** “always broken,” “broke for months,” “broken for half the semester,” “Usually at least 1 elevator is broken (3 total for 18 floors plus underground parking).”
- **Noise / thin walls — 3.** “Walls are paper thin”; “Thin walls”; “crazy loud birds outside.”

**Below the 3+ cut but worth knowing (1–2 voices, vivid):** hotel displacement for ~a month from year-long pool reno / water damage, with stuff broken when they returned; followed to a room and “security ain’t that great (there practically is none)”; dirty pool; silverfish; green fee / convenience fee / higher-floor charge (2 people — not a 3+ cluster).

**Why this is a strong video candidate**  
Elevators are the chorus, but the *pictures* are better than Ion-style one-liners: a pool under renovation that pushed people into a hotel, app-only door access (“annoying if your phone dies”), a thin-wall / no-security building. Easy to shoot elevator banks, the pool deck, and the parking stack in one visit.

**Good / bad / vibe**

- **Good:** Several “pretty nice / chill / calm spot”; rooms described as decent; gym / study / amenity room exist; Smart Housing discount mentioned; “no bugs” in one note (contradicted by silverfish in another).
- **Bad:** Chronic elevator outages; paper-thin walls; nickel-and-dime fees; pool dirty or under renovation all year; weak security; internet / Quext app complaints.
- **Vibe:** Mid-luxury that does not quite work. Less “health emergency” than Rise or The Nine; more “this is what the brochure left out.” Strong if you want a fees + elevators + security package rather than one crisis.

### 4. The Nine at Rio

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hood** | `The Nine at Rio` |
| **Notes / people** | 20 non-empty notes from **20 people**. 7 Wampus-only → only **13 people** with real resident text. Thinnest usable sample in the top 5. |

**Recurring issues (3+ people)**

- **Elevators — 7.** “Elevators always down”; “went 2 weeks without a single elevator”; “elevators don’t really work.”
- **Mold / water damage — 4.** This is the rare mold cluster that actually clears 3+.  
  - “mold issues, apartment smells horrible and hallways are disgusting, super dirty”  
  - “Was displaced for a month due to a severe mold infestation. Management is uncooperative and no compensation has been offered despite attempting legal recourse.”  
  - two additional notes that are just “mold” / “Mold”

**Why this is a strong video candidate**  
Mold plus a month of displacement is the most filmable *health* story in the file, and it stacks with a two-week total elevator outage. Volume is the weakness — a lot of the 20 notes are Wampus templates — so you will need to recruit on site rather than lean on the spreadsheet for color.

**Good / bad / vibe**

- **Good:** Gym / pool / patio / grill get mentioned; one person says elevators are working; huge patio (with a dark interior-facing window on a lower floor).
- **Bad:** Mold (including displacement + no compensation); hallways described as disgusting; trash overflowing; high humidity; slow maintenance; elevators down for weeks.
- **Vibe:** Smaller, darker, damper than the Union/Rise amenity towers. If Elijah wants one building where the camera can linger on hallways and moisture — and residents have a reason to go on record — this is it. Skip it if you need a deep bench of talkers from the notes alone.

### 5. Yugo Austin Waterloo

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hood** | `Yugo Austin Waterloo` |
| **Notes / people** | 23 non-empty notes from **23 people**. 2 Wampus-only + 4 one-letter junk (`a` / `A` / `G` / `Hy`) excluded → **17 people** with usable text. |

**Recurring issues (3+ people)**

- **Elevators — 7.** Not just “broken”: stuck / “sometimes drop several floors”; first semester down to 2 of 4 (sometimes 1 of 4) for ~3 months; 25th-floor waits while cars fill up on 30–20.
- **Room size / no-window — 5.** “extremely tiny bedrooms”; “Really small rooms and living room just not useful”; windowless rooms.
- **Pricing / hidden fees — 4.** $35/month green fee; premium-floor fee; charged if the trash room is overfilled “even if it wasn’t you”; hidden / application fees. (A fifth note says “No hidden fees” — not counted as a complaint.)

**Why this is a strong video candidate**  
This is the luxury-vs-operations building. Residents describe waterfall counters, a 30th-floor rooftop pool, saunas, Luxer — and elevators that drop floors, a garage so tight “every single car has scrapes down the side,” and rooms that feel like an afterthought. That contrast is a ready-made open.

**Good / bad / vibe**

- **Good:** Amenities list is the strongest in this set (rooftop pool, gym, yoga, saunas, cycling, free printing, package fridge); views / floor-to-ceiling windows; some $100/month concessions; elevators “fine ever since” after the 3-month outage (one long note).
- **Bad:** Elevators stuck or dropping; months with 1–2 of 4 running; tiny / windowless rooms; green fee + trash-room fine + premium floor; broken furniture / gym equipment; AC out in some rooms; thin walls; delivery system called “shitty.”
- **Vibe:** Instagram building, student-scale bedrooms, mechanical drama in year one. Best if you want garage scrapes + elevator-lobby waits + amenity-deck B-roll in one afternoon.

## How to pick 3 of these 5

| If you want… | Shoot |
|---|---|
| The most voices on one concrete failure | **Rise at West Campus** (water) |
| The best single walk-through contrast | **Tradition on Rio** (pretty lobby / roaches / dead elevators) |
| A health / displacement story | **The Nine at Rio** (mold) — recruit on site |
| Fees + security + a hotel subplot | **Signature 1909** |
| Luxury amenity deck vs tiny rooms / scraped garage | **Yugo Austin Waterloo** |

Safe default trio: **Rise**, **Tradition**, **Yugo** (volume + stacked themes + pictures). Swap Yugo for **The Nine** if mold is the piece you want in the cut.
